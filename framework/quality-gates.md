# Quality Gates

## SPEC-BLOCKER

Block progression when an essential requirement, constraint, ownership decision, dependency, acceptance condition, security/privacy condition, or operational assumption is missing or contradictory. Record the blocker, impact, owner, and unblock evidence.

## READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION

Pass only when the selected scope is understood, requirements are testable, decisions are recorded, necessary architecture and NFR views exist, dependencies and risks are visible, tasks are ordered, acceptance evidence is defined, and no unresolved SPEC-BLOCKER remains.

## CONVERGENCE

Pass when implementation evidence matches the accepted requirements and decisions, tests cover the agreed behavior and quality attributes, deviations are recorded, and operational/documentation evidence is sufficient.

Gates are evidence thresholds, not ceremonies. A project may use fewer gates only if the risk rationale is explicit.
