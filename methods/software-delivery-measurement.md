# Software Delivery Measurement

## Primary source
https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/

## VERIFIED FACT
DORA currently describes five software-delivery performance metrics grouped around throughput and instability. DORA's guidance emphasizes measuring an application/service in its own context and using the measures for improvement.

## Framework boundary
Delivery metrics are not individual productivity scores. They describe characteristics/outcomes of a delivery system and require contextual interpretation.

## Framework use
Use delivery measures as evidence for:
- bottleneck discovery;
- flow/stability trends;
- before/after improvement assessment;
- forecasting inputs where historical throughput is relevant.

Do not collapse product value, reliability, engineering quality and individual performance into one synthetic score without a validated purpose/model.
