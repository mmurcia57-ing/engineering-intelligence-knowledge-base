# Uptrace

Primary source: https://github.com/uptrace/uptrace

## VERIFIED FACT
Uptrace is an OpenTelemetry-based APM with a single UI for traces, metrics and logs. It documents service graphs built from span relationships, chart annotations, dashboards and alerting.

## Relevance
Useful evidence for:
- deriving service relationships from trace/span evidence;
- coordinating graph and time-series/chart annotations;
- retaining a unified signal exploration surface.

## Disposition
**ADAPT.** The graph derivation and annotation concepts are relevant; dashboard-centric structures are not automatically transferable.
